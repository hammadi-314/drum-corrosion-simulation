Interpretation and limitations

What is actually calculated? Mode 1 maps published or converted uniform corrosion benchmarks to a common visual scale. Mode 2 uses one fixed absolute color scale for all five materials in the selected rate scenario and exposure duration; each CAD face still receives its assumed local corrosion rate. Chamber-wall loss includes both sides. A gamma of 0.70 increases color contrast without changing numerical results.

What is not calculated? No H₂S electrochemical solution, material-specific kinetics, fluid pooling, pit initiation probability, crack growth, or structural stresses/deflections. All 10 CAD bodies are treated as exposed; high-rate zones are hypothetical and not predicted. Do not extrapolate the 7-day rate to real service life or claim sour-service qualification.

The source 316L study reported a deepest pit of 0.04681 mm after seven days. Spatial hotspot positions here remain hypothetical. Mode 1 study conditions differ, so color differences are not proof of better sour performance. Mode 2 uses a consistent scale across materials, but its rates and hotspot locations remain hypothetical. Colors are numerically comparable only within the same mode, scenario, duration and displayed field.
