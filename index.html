Interpretation and limitations

What is actually calculated? Local rates from an assigned 316L experimental benchmark are mapped to the imported triangulated STEP surfaces. Depth is computed as rate × (days/365); nominal remaining wall is 10 mm − depth. The minimum and maximum are calculated across the wetted triangle samples in the selected components.

What is not calculated? No H₂S electrochemical solution, material-specific kinetics, fluid pooling, pit initiation probability, crack growth, or structural stresses/deflections. The hottest locations are illustrative, not independently predicted. Do not extrapolate the 7-day rate to real service life or claim sour-service qualification.

The measured deepest pit in the original 316L experiment was 0.04681 mm after seven days. Your displayed per-face maximum is close but not exactly equal because it comes from discretized CAD hotspot interpolation, rather than the actual measured pit coordinates.
