Interpretation and limitations

What is actually calculated? Both modes use one published or transparently calculated corrosion-rate benchmark per candidate. Mode 1 applies it uniformly across the CAD. Mode 2 uses a shared illustrative hotspot field (not observed for these drum faces) and a fixed numerical color scale across five materials. Linear penetration = rate × days/365; chamber-wall estimates subtract both faces. The colors use gamma 0.70 for visibility only.

What is not calculated? No H₂S electrochemical solution, material-specific kinetics, fluid pooling, pit initiation probability, crack growth, or structural stresses/deflections. All 10 CAD bodies are treated as exposed; high-rate zones are hypothetical and not predicted. Do not extrapolate the 7-day rate to real service life or claim sour-service qualification.

Research basis and limitations: Source studies differ in pH, electrolyte, temperature, presence of H₂S and specimen grade/finish. Only the 316L source has the shown 3-bar H₂S exposure. An additional 316L paper reported 2.4 mm/year deepest-pit penetration; Mode 2 uses its ratio to the separate 0.74 mm/year uniform result as an unvalidated illustrative multiplier for other materials, not a measured pit rate or location. Neither mode establishes a suitable oil-and-gas material.
