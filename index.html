Engineering interpretation for the senior design report

Not H₂S service

The external magazine must be assessed for marine atmospheric exposure. The 3D field does not predict actual corrosion or material failure. Confirm site wetting, exposure, drainage, coating selection and crevice configuration before selecting a material.
